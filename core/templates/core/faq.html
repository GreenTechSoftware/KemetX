New here? Here are the basics about working, making, and finding your people on KemetX.

THE KEMETX COMMUNITY

What is KemetX?

KemetX is a community marketplace for Egyptian independent workers and makers. Clients can post projects, freelancers can submit proposals, and makers can list handmade products.

Who can join KemetX?

Anyone can create an account as a client, freelancer, or maker. Choose the role that best describes how you want to use the platform.

How do freelance projects work?

Clients publish project briefs with a budget in EGP. Freelancers can browse open projects and send one proposal per project with a note and their bid. Clients can review proposals and choose a freelancer from their dashboard.

How can I sell handmade products?

Create an account with the Maker role, then visit the Makers’ Market and use “List your work” to add a description, category, price, and city for your piece.

How are marketplace purchases paid?

Marketplace checkout is handled by Paymob in Egyptian pounds. Payment only becomes confirmed after KemetX receives and verifies Paymob’s signed server callback. Checkout is available once Paymob merchant credentials are configured.

Does KemetX charge a fee?

Fees and escrow arrangements are not yet configured. Confirm commercial and payment terms with KemetX before accepting or paying for work.

How do I get help?

For questions about your account or a listing, contact the KemetX team at hello@kemetx.com.
